Privacy Support

Support

Start with the checks below. When reporting a problem, include the extension version, browser version, the action you selected, and the exact error shown in Activity History.

An Archive Did Not Restore

  1. Open the Hibernated page and confirm the entry is still present.
  2. Retry Restore from the full page rather than the popup.
  3. Export the archive before clearing or reinstalling anything.

A Tab Stayed Open

Check Activity History for the exact protection or URL reason. Pinned, audible, media-playing, modified-form, captured, excluded-site, private, and unsupported tabs may remain open depending on Settings.

Private Tabs

Enable Allow in Incognito from the browser's extension details page, then choose the desired private-tab behavior in HTWS Settings. Private tabs stay open by default.

Before Reinstalling

Export the hibernation archive, Activity History, and Never-Hibernate site rules. Removing an extension also removes its local archive data.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the Chrome Web Store listing. Do not include private URLs or exported archive files unless they are necessary to diagnose the problem and you are comfortable sharing them.
